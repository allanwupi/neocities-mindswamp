## Thoughts on EEE at UWA
1. [Lu Jin](https://research-repository.uwa.edu.au/en/persons/lu-jin/) is the goat of electrical engineering at UWA. He lectures and facilitates ENSC2003 (which runs both semesters), ELEC3015, ELEC4404, and used to teach ELEC4505 (seen in past lecture recordings). In 2026 he also taught the first few workshops of GENG3402 for some reason.
2. I took CITS1401, a Computer Science unit, but normal engineering students take [CITS2401](https://www.handbooks.uwa.edu.au/unitdetails?code=CITS2401). I have a vague impression CITS2401 is harder (it covers more Python libraries like **matplotlib** and **numpy**), but I still don't know for sure.
3. Despite my good academic record, I am consistently terrible at studying for my electrical engineering exams. In semester 1 of 2026, I was pretty sure I failed the ELEC4404 and ELEC4505 exams, finishing about half the paper. I also struggle to believe I could have gotten a HD in ELEC4408. The simplest conclusion is that in higher-level engineering units, final exam marks get scaled heavily, but that's supposed to be against university policy...
4. MATLAB is a *terrible* programming language and we should all just use Python instead.
