## Summer Project 2026
As of 22 November 2026, the UWA Game Development Summer Project is officially underway. I have nominated myself as a developer; I will need to look at Godot and learning GDScript (unlike Python, it has manual memory management).

After much discussion, we have arrived at the following broad concepts for the game:
- Setting: flooded(?) post-apocalyptic world, "Underwater Academy" (UWA)
- Art: 3D low-poly graphics, 3rd-person, probably goofy
- Genre: multiplayer tower defense game, reasonably short playtime
    - During the day, explore surrounding buildings to gather resources and unlock upgrades
    - Before night falls, get back to your base at Reid Library and build defences (e.g. turrets, traps)
    - Survive the night as hordes of enemies/invasive species (to be determined) try to disrupt your studies
    - You have limited manpower with which to protect multiple buildings
    - End in a final boss battle after a fixed number of rounds (12 semesters?)
