I like to play board games. I am not very good at them, however.

## Leder Games
Leder Games is great, or maybe Cole Werle is great? They make asymmetric games. I own literally every Root expansion (neoprene mats arriving soon) and will buy every expansion they release.
1. [*Root: A Woodland Game of Might and Right*](https://rules.ledergames.com/)
2. [*Arcs: Conflict & Collapse in the Reach*](https://cmckenz87.github.io/ArcsFates/index.html)

Actually I should just make a section about Root. Maybe later.

I highly recommend watching [Nevakanezah's Root strategy guides](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TPm85Sf7ugA&list=PLgmddIIcKYDHVBCt0sItFzaJhRwkowkaO).

## Other Board Games
These games are also pretty good:
- *Air, Land & Sea*, and the standalone expansion *Spies, Lies & Supplies* (very small and quick game, 2 players)
- *The Old King's Crown* (up to 4 players, you can play it [online](https://oldkingscrown.fly.dev/))
  - I have ordered both expansions, estimated arrival May 2027

Some other board games I am excited about playing (pre-ordered or owned but haven't played yet):
- *An Infamous Traffic*, 2nd edition
- *High Frontier 4 All*
- *Plants vs. Zombies: The Board Game*

Games I haven't played very often and am still undecided about:
- *Nemesis* (quite a long game, a bit random and swingy)
- *Spirit Island* (complicated and requires effective teamwork)

Games I don't like very much:
- *Kelp: Shark vs Octopus* (it's not very balanced)
- *Root* at player counts other than 4-5 players
